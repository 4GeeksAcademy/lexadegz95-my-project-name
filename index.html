01 / About me

A different way of seeing.

I'm Sofie, a drone pilot drawn to the details that change when you see a place from above.

I create considered aerial imagery and live flight experiences, pairing careful planning with a curious eye. Every flight starts on the ground: understanding the place, its people, and the story worth bringing into view.

Curiosity, with a view.
