Product Context
Project Overview
This project is a single-page landing website for Sofie, a professional drone photographer and videographer.

Sofie uses drone photography and video to help businesses, real estate professionals, and sporting events capture unique aerial perspectives and create engaging visual content.

The goal of the website is to establish Sofie's online presence, showcase her work, explain her services, and make it easy for potential clients to contact her.

#Target Audience
The website is designed for:

Real estate agents and property owners looking for aerial photography and video
Businesses looking for short, high-quality aerial clips for websites, social media, and advertising
Sports teams, event organizers, and venues looking for aerial footage of live sporting events
Potential clients interested in professional drone photography and videography
Services
Real Estate
Aerial photography and cinematic video for residential and commercial properties. The goal is to give potential buyers a unique perspective of the property and surrounding area.

#Business Content
Short, visually appealing drone clips that businesses can purchase and use for:

Social media
Websites
Advertisements
Promotional videos
Marketing campaigns
Live Sporting Events
Dynamic aerial footage of sporting events that captures the scale, energy, and atmosphere of the event from a unique perspective.

#Website Structure
The website will be a single-page experience with sections that approximately fill the height of the screen.

#Navigation
The navigation will include:

Home
About Sofie
Services
Portfolio
Contact
Hero / Home
The first section will introduce Sofie and immediately communicate what she does.

Example messaging:

Sofie — Drone Photographer & Videographer Capturing the world from a different perspective.
Primary calls to action will encourage visitors to view Sofie's work or inquire about booking her services.

#About
This section will introduce Sofie, her passion for drone photography and videography, and her creative approach to capturing aerial content.

#Services
This section will explain Sofie's three primary services:

Real Estate
Business Video Content
Live Sporting Events
Portfolio
A visual showcase of Sofie's drone photography and video work. Portfolio content can be organized around her three main service categories.

#Contact
A simple section designed to turn visitors into potential clients. It will include a clear call-to-action and contact information.

#Design Direction
The visual style should feel:

Modern
Cinematic
Professional
Creative
Clean
Image-focused
Large photography and video should play an important role in the design because Sofie's work is highly visual.

The site should be responsive and provide a good experience on both desktop and mobile devices.

#Accessibility
Accessibility is an important requirement of the project.

The website should use:

Semantic HTML elements
Descriptive alt text for meaningful images
Accessible navigation
Proper heading hierarchy
Sufficient color contrast
Clearly labeled buttons and links
Keyboard-friendly interactions
Accessible form labels where applicable
The goal is to make the website usable by visitors with different visual and physical abilities, including people using screen readers.

#SEO
The website should follow basic SEO best practices so potential clients can discover Sofie through search engines.

This includes:

A descriptive page title
A useful meta description
Semantic HTML
Proper heading structure
Descriptive image alt attributes
Relevant keywords used naturally throughout the content
Descriptive URLs and link text where applicable
Schema.org structured data using JSON-LD
Schema.org markup can help search engines understand who Sofie is, what services she provides, and what the website represents.
